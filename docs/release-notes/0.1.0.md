First release of Mac Stats, a menu bar monitor for macOS 14 Sonoma and later (Apple silicon and Intel).

## What it does

- Six metrics: CPU, memory, network, GPU, disk I/O and disk usage, with live values and history up to 30 days (7 or 90 if you choose).
- Each item can sit in the menu bar, in the menu only, or both. Five menu bar styles: number, chart, chart + number, gauge, two values.
- Click an item for its menu: period chips (1m to 30d), a chart with scale numbers, detail rows, and breakdowns by process, core, interface, disk or volume.
- Every menu ends with **Other metrics**. Hover one to open its full menu to the side.
- Or switch to the **overview**: one card per item; click a card for that item's menu. ⌥-click opens the other view.
- Chart values on hover (off by default), light and dark appearance, and a settings window with export and import.

## Defaults

CPU, Memory and Network are in the menu bar. All six metrics are in the menu. See `docs/kb/configuration.md`.

## Known limits

- This build is **ad-hoc signed and not notarized**. On first launch, right-click the app and choose **Open**, or run
  `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Mac Stats.app"`.
- No auto-update yet. New versions appear on this Releases page.
- Per-app network (`nettop`) and per-app GPU (undocumented IORegistry keys) are experimental.
- Processes owned by other users (root) are shown together as one line.
- Temperature, fans and public IP are not included.
